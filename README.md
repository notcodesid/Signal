# Signal

## Brief

Embedded AI in all crypto protocols.

An ambient layer (extension or browser) embedded into crypto protocols that suggests DeFi strategies, launches assets, and executes on-chain actions in-flow.

## Problem

Just like current SaaS products such as Figma or Shopify, most crypto protocols will become more AI-native. AI should be contextual, ambient, and proactive—right in the flow of trading and other crypto actions.

## Solution

An ambient layer (extension or browser) embedded across crypto protocols that understands your intent: suggesting DeFi strategies such as yield looping, launching assets, and executing on-chain actions directly in your workflow.
